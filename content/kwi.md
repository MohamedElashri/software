An independently maintained macOS fork of Kwi, the terminal emulator from KDE, built for Apple Silicon Macs with Qt Quick, KDE Frameworks and libghostty-vt. It adds proper handling of Arabic, Persian and Hebrew text, and upstream KDE Kwi changes are reviewed and brought in regularly.

- Tabbed sessions with searchable scrollback and keyboard selection
- Bidirectional rendering and letter joining for Arabic, Persian and Hebrew, including mixed-script copy and search
- An option to turn off bidirectional rendering for programs that arrange right-to-left text themselves
- Familiar Mac shortcuts, with optional Option-as-Alt
- Launch presets, color schemes, fonts and configurable shortcuts
