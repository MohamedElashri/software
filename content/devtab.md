A Firefox extension that replaces the new tab page with a feed of developer news. Requests go straight to each source's public API, with no tracking, no ads and no backend server in between.

- News from GitHub, Hacker News, Lobsters, Product Hunt, Reddit and your own RSS feeds
- Tag filters and card controls for organizing feeds
- Bookmarks and read state kept across sessions
- Do Not Disturb hours, dark and light themes and a compact mode
